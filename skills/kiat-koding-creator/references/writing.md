# Writing style for Kiat Koding lessons

Lesson content is in Indonesian, written to the learner as a peer would explain it: clear, warm, direct. These rules hold for course info, lessons, codelab steps, briefs, milestones and hints.

## Rules

- Address the learner as "kamu" and "-mu", never "Anda".
- No semicolons. Join clauses with a comma or split them with a period.
- No em dashes (—), and no double hyphens used as dashes. Use a comma, a period, a colon or parentheses.
- "Submission", never "tugas". "project", never "proyek".
- Technical terms stay in English: file names, identifiers, commands, error messages, tool and product names. Technical verbs keep the English word with Indonesian affixes, the way Indonesian developers talk: "di-commit", "di-deploy", "nge-parse", "me-return", "di-install". Avoid stiff translations such as "mengembalikan" for return, "berkas" for file, "tautan" for link, "peladen" for server.
- No hype or filler: "luar biasa", "revolusioner", "dengan mudah", "secara efektif", "tingkatkan skill-mu ke level berikutnya".
- No slogan lines that balance two halves ("Kamu yang menentukan arah, agent yang menulis kodenya") and no "bukan sekadar X, tapi Y".
- No forced lists of three where the content has two or four.
- Bold sparingly, for a term at its definition or one warning. Not for every keyword.
- Captions are plain sentences about what the image shows. Never "Label: text", never "Bagian yang terang …".
- Never invent numbers, quotes, testimonials or results.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Pada langkah ini, Anda akan mempelajari cara membuat endpoint; endpoint ini sangat penting. | Di langkah ini kamu bikin endpoint pertama. Endpoint ini yang nanti dipanggil aplikasi mobile. |
| Fungsi ini mengembalikan nilai boolean. | Fungsi ini me-return boolean. |
| Kerjakan tugas berikut — pastikan proyekmu rapi. | Kerjakan Submission berikut, dan pastikan project-mu rapi. |
| *Gambar: Halaman login.* | *Halaman login setelah token-nya kedaluwarsa.* |
| Dengan teknik ini, kamu bisa dengan mudah meningkatkan performa aplikasi secara signifikan! | Dengan cache ini, halaman produk tidak lagi memanggil database di setiap request. |

## Tone

Explain like a senior developer sitting next to the learner: say why before how, name the trap before the learner falls into it, and admit when something is tedious. Short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph.
